OBITO is BEST

Obito Uchiha was once a
boy who dreamed of becoming
Hokage, a boy who believed that
love and friendship could change
the world. But life took away
everything he held dear,
leaving behind a heart full of pain
and a world he could no longer believe in.
He wasn't born a villain;
he was a broken soul who lost
his way while searching for a place
without suffering. Behind his mask
was a boy who once smiled, a friend
who once cared, and a dream that slowly
turned into darkness. Obito didn't lose
himself in a single moment; he lost
himself piece by piece, until the world forgot
the boy he used to be. 🖤🥀
